Who we are

Built by a student,
for every student.

Too many young Pakistanis miss out on scholarships, programs, and opportunities — not because they aren't qualified, but because the information simply isn't reaching them. It's scattered, hard to find, and rarely shared in accessible ways.

GatewayPK is a youth-led initiative to change that. We curate verified opportunities and make them free, accessible, and easy to act on — regardless of your city or background.

This is Version 1. Small start. Big vision.

Accessibility

Opportunities should be visible to everyone, not just those with connections.

Accuracy

Every listing is verified before it goes live. No spam, no outdated posts.

Community

This platform grows with student feedback, suggestions, and contributions.

Action

We don't just list things — we help students apply and succeed.

"Information asymmetry is one of the biggest barriers for Pakistani youth. GatewayPK is here to close that gap."
— GatewayPK, founded 2026
